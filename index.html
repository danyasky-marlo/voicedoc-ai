Features

Built for people who talk faster than they type.

Three ideas, one frictionless workflow.

whisper → llm

Speech to structured data

Whisper transcribes every word. A language model then understands the meaning, pulls out names, numbers, dates and addresses, and shapes them into clean fields.

  • Whisper-grade transcription
  • LLM entity extraction
  • Output as fields, rows or text
ctrl+shift+space

Global hotkey & integration

Press one shortcut anywhere in Windows or macOS, speak, and release. The result lands exactly where your cursor is — Word, Excel, a browser form or any desktop app.

  • Works system-wide
  • Fills fields, tables and documents
  • Fully customizable shortcut

How it works

Three seconds from thought to text.

01

Press & speak

Hold your hotkey and say it the way you’d tell a colleague. No commands, no templates to memorize.

02

AI structures it

Speech is transcribed, then analyzed. Key data is identified and mapped to the right fields.

03

Done in place

Release the key — the data is typed into your document, form or table. Review, adjust, move on.

Privacy

Veloce Voice respects your privacy. Here’s how your voice and data are handled.

  • Encrypted audio streams

    Your voice is sent over encrypted connections, so it can’t be intercepted on the way.

  • Processed securely, never stored

    Audio and transcripts are used only to produce your result and are not stored after processing.

  • Minimal by design

    We handle only what’s needed to fill your document. No ads, and we never sell your data.

This is a short summary for the v0.1 beta; a full policy will be published before public release.
